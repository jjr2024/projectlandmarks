9 Add is_last_minute flag to digital/instant-delivery items Needs Revision ›

The column exists and is populated, but incorrectly: 65 of 67 items (97%) are marked is_last_minute = yes. This defeats the entire purpose. The flag was meant to identify digital/instant-delivery items that are still good picks with ≤2 days notice — things like e-gift cards, digital subscriptions, or downloadable content. Marking physical Amazon products as last-minute is wrong.

With 97% flagged, the +10 last-minute bonus is nearly universal and the -20 penalty almost never fires. The scoring distinction is meaningless.
Effort: Small. Audit the 67 items, set most physical products to "no", keep "yes" only for truly instant-delivery items. Currently the catalog may have very few genuinely digital items, so this might also require adding some.
New Suggestions (from this audit)
A Fix the category mismatch between DB seed and webapp — items are unreachable Critical ›

This is the operational consequence of #1 being unfinished. Right now, if the gift_catalog was seeded from the XLS as-is, items with categories like "treat", "food", "clothing", "shoes", "beauty", "jewelry", "cooking", "health", "music", "toys & games" would never be served because the engine filters on exact category match against user-selected webapp categories. You need either a category mapping layer in the engine or — simpler — align the XLS categories to the webapp categories before seeding.

This should be resolved before any catalog re-seed. It's the single biggest data quality issue.
B Add custom icons for Books, Electronics, Sports in gift-icons.tsx Minor ›

These three categories were added in the previous session but gift-icons.tsx has no icons for them — they fall back to a generic gift icon. Small polish item.

C Fix the fallback categories in gift-engine.ts Important ›

When a contact has no gift_categories set, the engine defaults to ["flowers", "gift_card"]. Since "gift_card" has zero catalog items, this fallback is half-broken. Should be changed to categories with actual catalog coverage, e.g., ["flowers", "home"] or a broader default set.

D Consider adding more genuinely digital/instant-delivery items to the catalog Nice to Have ›

Once is_last_minute is corrected (suggestion #9), you may find you have very few truly last-minute items. The engine's last-minute logic is well-built (+10 bonus, -20 penalty, broadened query), but it needs actual digital products to work with — streaming subscriptions, e-gift cards, digital magazine subscriptions, online course passes, etc.
